Guide

Welcome to your Roadster ♥
Drive it, fly it, show it off. Tap a topic below.
Open this screen inside the car to control the lights
🚪Getting in
  • Driver: right-click the car and choose Drive.
  • Passenger: click the passenger seat, or right-click it and choose Sit.
  • To get out, click Stand Up.
  • The camera follows you automatically while you drive.
🚗Driving
W↑Accelerate
S↓Brake, then reverse
AD←→Steer
CPgDnExtra brake
  • Steering works once you're rolling, just like a real car.
  • Let go of the keys and the car gently coasts to a stop.
✈️Flying
Take off: hold the left mouse button for about 1.5 seconds, or type /7 fly in local chat. Wait a few seconds after sitting down before you try.
W↑Speed up
S↓Slow down / reverse
ADTurn (the car banks)
EPgUpClimb
CPgDnGo lower
🛬Landing
  • Fly low and hold C. The car lands itself near the ground.
  • Or type /7 land and it sets down gently below you.
  • Standing up mid-air? The car lowers itself safely to the ground.
🚪Doors
  • Touch the left or right door to open it. Touch again to close.
  • Each door opens on its own, so you can make a grand entrance.
✦Lights
  • Use the Lights app on this screen for colours, effects, glow and speed.
  • Console light panel: tap the left side for ambient lights, the middle for the cabin light, the right side for headlights.
  • Hold the panel for 1 second, or touch the dashboard, for the full lights menu.
  • 10 colours and 8 effects. Your favourite settings are remembered.
  • The owner and anyone sitting in the car can change the lights.
💺Seat fit

The owner, sitting in the driver's seat, can fine-tune both seats:

/7 seat up/7 seat down/7 seat fwd/7 seat back/7 seat reset
📏Bigger or smaller
  • Head poking through the roof? Make the car bigger! (owner only)
  • Everyone stands up first.
  • Type /7 size, or hold the left mouse button on the car body for 1.5 seconds.
  • Pick −5% · −1% · +1% · +5% or 100% · 110% · 120% · 130%. Or type an exact size, like /7 size 115.
  • Seats, poses and doors adjust with it. 115–120% fits taller avatars nicely.
🎨Paint colours
  • Rez the Tesla Colour Cube within 20 m of your car.
  • Touch the cube, pick your car, then pick a colour.
Stealth GreyPearl White Solid BlackDeep Blue

💡Little helpers
  • Car won't move? Some land doesn't allow vehicles. Try your own land or a vehicle-friendly sandbox.
  • Something feels stuck? Have everyone stand up, then sit again.
  • Upside down? Just stand up. The car flips back onto its wheels.
  • Can't take off? Wait a few seconds after sitting, then hold the left mouse a little longer, or type /7 fly.
  • Screen blank? Turn media on in your viewer (Preferences › Sound & Media), then touch the screen.
Enjoy the ride, and the sky ✈ ♥
